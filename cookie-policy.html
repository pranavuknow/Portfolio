Cookie Policy

Effective: 29 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 September 2026

This page explains what cookies and browser storage this site uses, and which third-party services may set cookies or make network requests when you visit. The short answer: this site sets no first-party cookies, runs no analytics, and places no advertising trackers.

1. Overview

Cookies are small text files placed in your browser by a website or a third-party service embedded on that site. They are commonly used for session management, analytics, advertising, and personalisation.

This portfolio website sets no first-party cookies whatsoever. There is no login system, no shopping cart, no analytics script, and no advertising network. The only browser storage this site writes is a single localStorage key, described in section 4.

No cookie consent banner is shown because this site does not set tracking or non-essential cookies. The information below is provided for full transparency about every external request the page makes.

2. Google Fonts (Third-Party Technical Request)

This site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. When your browser requests the font files from fonts.gstatic.com, it sends a standard HTTP request that includes your IP address. Google may log this request in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Google Fonts does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Font files may be cached by your browser after the first visit, which speeds up subsequent visits and means the request is not repeated. This caching is controlled by standard HTTP cache headers, not by cookie-based identifiers.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. YouTube Thumbnail CDN (i.ytimg.com)

Project thumbnails in the work portfolio are static images served from YouTube's image CDN at i.ytimg.com. When your browser loads a portfolio page, it fetches these images directly from Google's servers. This is a one-way image request: your IP address is sent to Google as part of the standard HTTP request, and the image file is returned.

No YouTube player loads on page load. No YouTube cookies are set while you are browsing the portfolio grid. If you click on a project card to watch a video, a YouTube embed is loaded inside the lightbox at that point. Once the YouTube player loads, YouTube's own cookies and tracking may apply, governed by Google's terms of service and privacy policy.

You can avoid triggering a YouTube embed entirely by not clicking the video play button.

4. Theme Preference (localStorage, Not a Cookie)

This site remembers whether you prefer the dark or light theme. This preference is stored as a single key in your browser's localStorage, under the key name pranav-theme. The value is either dark or light.

localStorage is not a cookie. Unlike cookies, it is not sent to the server with every request. It stays in your browser, on your device, and is read only by client-side JavaScript on this site. It contains no personal data and is not used for tracking.

Summary of all browser storage used by this site:

Key: pranav-theme  |  Type: localStorage  |  Value: dark or light  |  Purpose: theme preference  |  Personal data: none

5. Your Choices

Because this site sets no first-party cookies, there is nothing to opt out of on our end. If you wish to clear your theme preference, open your browser's developer tools, go to Application, then Local Storage, find the entry for pranav-theme, and delete it. The site will then revert to your system's default colour scheme preference on the next visit.

To block all third-party requests (including Google Fonts and the YouTube image CDN), you can use a browser extension such as uBlock Origin. Blocking Google Fonts will cause the site to render in your browser's system sans-serif font instead of Inter.
