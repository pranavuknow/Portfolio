Terms of Service

Effective: 29 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 September 2026

These terms govern the freelance services provided by Pranav Shukla (referred to as "Pranav" below). By commissioning a project, you (the client) agree to these terms. If anything here is unclear before you hire, please ask. Every project starts with a brief conversation, not a pile of paperwork.

1. Agreement

A working agreement is formed when both parties have confirmed the project scope, price, and timeline in writing, typically over email or a messaging platform. These terms apply to all such agreements. They are governed by the laws of India.

These terms do not replace a formal contract. For larger or ongoing engagements, a separate written agreement may be prepared and will take precedence over these terms where they conflict.

2. Services Described

Pranav offers the following creative services:

  • YouTube long-form video editing: Full narrative editing, retention pacing, color grading, sound design, and music placement for videos typically 8 to 60 minutes long.
  • Short-form content editing: High-retention vertical edits for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and similar platforms. Includes captions, pacing, and hook optimisation.
  • Thumbnail design: Click-worthy, mobile-readable custom thumbnails using Photoshop or equivalent tools, informed by CTR best practices.

The specific deliverables for a given project are agreed before work begins. If a requested task falls outside the agreed scope, it is treated as additional work and priced separately.

3. Project Scope and Deliverables

Scope is defined by the project brief shared at the start of work. This includes the type of content, approximate duration, style references, and any platform-specific requirements. Both parties must agree on scope before work begins.

Changes to scope requested after work has started, including added footage, format changes, or new deliverable types, may affect the timeline and price. Pranav will notify the client before proceeding with out-of-scope work.

4. Turnaround Times

Estimated turnaround times are communicated at project start. Standard turnaround for a long-form video edit is 24 to 48 hours from receipt of all raw assets, depending on complexity and current workload. These are estimates, not contractual guarantees.

Delays caused by late delivery of assets, unclear briefs, or extended revision cycles on the client side are not counted against Pranav's turnaround commitment. Rush delivery may be available for an additional fee, discussed case by case.

5. Revisions

Each project includes two rounds of revisions, as agreed upon during initial scoping discussion. A revision round means one consolidated set of feedback applied in a single pass. Scattered or incremental feedback that arrives over multiple messages is consolidated by Pranav into one revision cycle.

Revisions must stay within the agreed scope and style direction. Requests that constitute a change of direction, a new concept, or a significant structural recut are treated as a new scope item and may be priced separately.

If you are unsure whether a request counts as a revision or a scope change, ask before Pranav starts. A quick message saves time for both sides.

6. Client Responsibilities

To allow Pranav to deliver on time and to brief, the client is responsible for:

  • Providing all raw footage, assets, and brand guidelines before work begins.
  • Sharing clear references or style examples if a specific look is expected.
  • Providing consolidated, actionable feedback within the agreed revision window (typically 48 hours after delivery of a draft).
  • Making agreed payments on time.

Pranav cannot be held responsible for delays or quality issues arising from incomplete assets, late feedback, or a brief that was changed after work began.

7. Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of full payment, the client owns the final delivered files. Pranav transfers all editing and design work product to the client for their intended use. Source project files (DaVinci Resolve .drp, Premiere .prproj, Photoshop .psd) are not included in standard delivery unless explicitly agreed.

Pranav retains the right to display the completed work in his portfolio, on his website, and on his social media channels as examples of his craft, unless the client requests otherwise in writing before or during the project. Pranav will not share confidential or unreleased content without explicit permission.

The client is responsible for ensuring they hold the necessary rights to all footage, music, and other assets provided to Pranav. Pranav is not liable for any intellectual property claims arising from client-supplied material.

8. Payment Terms

Payment terms are agreed before work begins. The standard arrangement is a 50% deposit before the project starts and the remaining 50% upon delivery of the final files. For smaller single-deliverable projects, full payment upfront may be required.

Final files are not released until payment is complete. Deposits are non-refundable once work has begun. See the Refund Policy for full details on cancellations and disputes.

9. Limitation of Liability

Pranav's liability for any claim arising from a project is limited to the amount paid for that project. Pranav is not liable for indirect losses, including lost revenue, missed publishing deadlines, or damage to brand reputation.

This limitation applies to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law in India.

10. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising from a project that cannot be resolved by mutual agreement will be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts in India.
