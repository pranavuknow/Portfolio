Active Cryptographic & Security Architecture

Shield Active

1. Zero-Plaintext Storage: Passphrases are salted with pranav_cinema_salt_9988 and verified using asynchronous crypto.subtle.digest('SHA-256') with constant-time byte comparison.

2. Brute-Force Rate Limiter: Automatically enforces a 15-minute lockout upon 5 consecutive failed authentication attempts.
