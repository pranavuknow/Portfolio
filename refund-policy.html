Refund Policy

Effective: 29 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 September 2026

Freelance creative work involves significant time invested before a deliverable is visible. This policy explains how deposits, cancellations, and quality disputes are handled. The goal is to be fair to both sides. When something goes wrong, the first step is always a direct conversation.

1. Nature of Creative Services

Every project Pranav works on is custom creative work: video editing, short-form content, or thumbnail design produced specifically for the client's brief. Because this work is bespoke and cannot be "returned" or resold, it does not fit the refund model that applies to physical or off-the-shelf digital products.

This does not mean client concerns are dismissed. If a deliverable falls short of what was agreed, Pranav will work to resolve it. The sections below describe exactly how.

Refunds are not offered once work has commenced. Disputes about quality are resolved through additional revisions or, where appropriate, a partial credit toward future work.

2. Deposits

A deposit, typically 50% of the agreed project fee, is required before work begins. Paying a deposit confirms the project brief, timeline, and price. Once work has started, the deposit is non-refundable.

The deposit covers the time spent reviewing the brief, planning the edit, and beginning production. This time cannot be recovered if a project is cancelled mid-way.

If a project has not yet started and a deposit has been paid, the deposit may be refunded at Pranav's discretion, depending on whether any preparation time has already been invested.

3. Deliverable Disputes

If a delivered file significantly deviates from the agreed scope and brief, the client should raise the issue within 5 business days of delivery. Pranav will review the brief against the deliverable and, if the concern is valid, offer one or more of the following at his discretion:

  • Additional revision rounds to bring the deliverable into alignment with the brief.
  • A partial credit toward a future project, if the scope deviation is substantial and cannot be resolved through revision.

Disputes based on subjective preferences that were not specified in the original brief (for example, "I expected a different style" when no style reference was provided) are handled through the standard revision process, not as a quality dispute.

Pranav aims to resolve all disputes by mutual agreement. If an agreement cannot be reached, both parties may seek resolution under the laws of India.

4. Cancellations

Either party may cancel a project before the first draft is delivered. The following terms apply:

  • Cancelled before first draft delivery: The deposit is retained by Pranav. The remaining balance, if any has been invoiced, is waived. No further payment is owed by the client.
  • Cancelled after first draft delivery: The full project fee is owed. The client receives the first draft as delivered. No further revision rounds are included.

Cancellations must be communicated in writing by email to pranav.uknow@gmail.com. Verbal cancellations are not binding until confirmed in writing.

5. Contact for Disputes

If you have a concern about a deliverable or a payment, the first step is to email Pranav directly. Most issues can be resolved with one conversation.

  • Email: pranav.uknow@gmail.com
  • Subject line: "Project Dispute: [your project name or date]"

Pranav will acknowledge dispute emails within 48 hours and aim to propose a resolution within 5 business days.
