Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data is collected when you visit this portfolio website (https://pranavuknow.github.io/Portfolio/), how it is used, and what rights you have over it. This is a personal freelance portfolio website operated by Pranav Shukla, an individual based in India. There is no company, no analytics platform, and no advertising network involved.

1. Who We Are

This website is owned and operated by Pranav Shukla, a freelance video editor and thumbnail designer based in India. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, Pranav Shukla acts as the Data Fiduciary for any personal data received through direct email correspondence with enquirers.

Contact: pranav.uknow@gmail.com
Website: https://pranavuknow.github.io/Portfolio/

2. What Data We Collect

This site does not run a backend server, does not operate a database, and does not store any form submissions. The contact form on this site generates an email draft that opens in your own Gmail account. You send the email yourself, from your own address, to Pranav.

The fields the form pre-populates into that email draft are:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The service type you selected
  • Your budget range
  • A project link (optional)
  • A short project description

None of this data passes through or is stored by this website. You retain full control: you can edit or discard the draft before sending. Once you send the email, Pranav receives it in his personal Gmail inbox and that email becomes the only record of your enquiry.

3. Why We Collect It

The only purpose for which Pranav holds personal data is to respond to service enquiries. If you send an email, that email is read and replied to. No data is used for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose.

The legal basis under the DPDP Act 2023 is consent: you initiate contact voluntarily, and you choose to send the email from your own email client.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)

This site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches font files, it sends a standard HTTP request to Google's servers. That request includes your IP address as a technical side effect of how the internet works. Google may log this request. No tracking cookies are set by Google Fonts, and font files are not used to identify you between visits.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

YouTube Image CDN (i.ytimg.com)

Project thumbnails in the work portfolio are displayed as static images. These images are loaded directly from YouTube's image CDN (i.ytimg.com). This is a standard image request: your browser fetches the image file, and your IP address is sent to Google's servers as part of that request.

No YouTube player loads on page load. No YouTube cookies are set unless you actively click a project card to open a video in the lightbox, at which point a YouTube embed is loaded and YouTube's own terms and cookie policy apply.

This site uses no analytics services, no advertising networks, and no social media pixels. Beyond the two technical CDN requests described above, no data about your visit is shared with any third party.

5. Cookies

This site sets no first-party cookies. See the Cookie Policy for a full explanation, including how Google Fonts may cache font files and how the theme preference is stored in localStorage, not in a cookie.

6. How Long We Keep Data

Email correspondence is kept for as long as a business relationship is active or reasonably expected to resume. If a project is completed and no ongoing relationship exists, enquiry emails are typically deleted within 12 months.

You may request deletion of your email correspondence at any time by writing to pranav.uknow@gmail.com. Pranav will respond within 72 hours.

7. Your Rights Under India's DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), you have the following rights regarding any personal data Pranav holds about you in email correspondence:

  • Right to access: You may request a summary of what personal data is held.
  • Right to correction: You may request that inaccurate data be corrected.
  • Right to erasure: You may request that your data be deleted, subject to any legal obligation to retain records.
  • Right to grievance redressal: If your request is not addressed within a reasonable time, you may raise a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted.

To exercise any of these rights, email pranav.uknow@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDP Data Request". Requests will be acknowledged within 48 hours and acted upon within 30 days.

8. Contact for Data Requests

For any question or request relating to this privacy policy or your personal data, contact Pranav Shukla directly:

  • Email: pranav.uknow@gmail.com
  • Subject line for data requests: "DPDP Data Request"

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Minor editorial corrections may be made without notice. For significant changes, a notice may be posted on the main site for 30 days.
